## Documented look / fix / recheck

Direction remains Accent ladder (877116420), retained green/IBM type. App-screen critique rules apply; marketing photography/motion quotas do not. No fake commercial photos or motion were introduced into event operations.

Before: `.taste-skill/stage4-before/` contains desktop/tablet/mobile folds and full-page sheets plus the inspector report. Skill meter already reported zero failures, while pictures clearly showed the diagnostics dominating first use. A clean meter was not treated as good UX.

Round 1: `.taste-skill/stage4-round1-light/` and `stage4-round1-dark/` passed the skill browser measurements at 1440/768/390. All six fold images were opened. First impression: real event actions now lead; restrained typography/green is coherent. The biggest remaining issues were repeated mobile metrics, insufficient separation of organizer self-approval, and a native WebKit select target. Those were corrected rather than accepting the first render.

Round 2/3: populated screenshots under `.cache/stage4/screens/{chromium,webkit}/` cover Overview, guest list, tickets, devices, attendance, scanning and exchange in both themes, at 320/390/768/1440 and 720×450 layout-equivalent zoom. Final inspection details/results are in STAGE_4_TEST_RESULTS.md. Subjective app critique: hierarchy is clearer; short real lists/zeros stay honest; one consistent gutter/tint system; no chart warrants adding. Enrollment remains wordy because the independent trust ceremony is a real security requirement. This is not a claim that novices now complete it unassisted.

The later supplied task-dashboard reference prompted actual ticket-issuance progress and received-admission panels. A sticky top bar introduced during that refinement obscured part of an actual enrollment screenshot: the retained decoder regression failed. Removed the sticky top bar rather than weakening decode assertions, QR geometry or signature checks. The sidebar can remain stable without covering optical targets.

Final review questions: Is each screen's next action visible? Do paired-device instructions name both roles? Do totals agree with rows? Do missing and failed storage reads stay distinct? Are screenshots readable at narrow widths? Do QR quiet zones remain intact? Automated results and manual gaps are recorded separately in the test report. No independent design jury score is claimed.

## Final app critique · self-review, not a user-study score

Seen in one second: EntryLoop, the current event, its real preparation state and the next action. Idea: prepare entrances, admit signed tickets and exchange evidence when the network is unavailable. Live interaction: actual issuance, approval handoffs and durable verified exchange. Layout: events = dated peer objects; overview = local metrics / next action / issuance and setup / event context / received admissions; guests = actions, search and named rows; scanner = event, camera and local result; exchange = two-device handoff and evidence.

Question/hierarchy: the next preparation action leads Overview; task totals collapse outside it so they do not dominate guests/tickets. Edges: equal desktop/mobile gutters and contained scrolling tabs/tables; no QR overlays. Color: restrained green tint roles and textual success/rejection, no decorative charts. Numbers: overview totals, issuance ratio and row states come from the same stored inputs; zero-guests has no invented progress denominator. Empty/unknown: genuine zero, read failure, no authorization and preserved evidence stay separate. Phone: navigation folds, controls remain at least 44px and guest rows stack. Remaining weakness: enrollment and large optical transfers still require significant human effort; the independent full-key trust step cannot be removed by a design-only change. Human comprehension, cameras, real browser zoom and screen-reader behavior are unverified. No numeric jury/usability score is asserted.

Final skill checks: healthy empty screen reports zero failures/warnings at 1440/768/390 in both themes. The skill's actual DOM probes on 42 UI-populated app views report zero failures; two warnings concern a horizontally scrolled Event details tab sitting 15px from the mobile edge. This is contained tab navigation, not page overflow; the full control remains focusable and reachable. Production source lint excludes test fixtures and reports zero failures; the remaining monospace warning is advanced signed transport/IDs, and the em-dash warning aggregates separate technical status screens. No rule was hidden with an allowlist or application markup exception.

Final fresh-browser review: role choices now precede technical origin/build/name controls. The missing-event warning remains visible, and enumeration failures never receive a fresh default workspace action. `.taste-skill/stage4-first-use-{light,dark}/` reports zero failures/warnings at all three skill widths; all listed images were opened. At 320px, the browser regression checks both setup actions remain near the top and confirms choosing either view creates zero organizer/scanner signing identities. Existing-workspace selection and failed-inspection guidance retain their original safe behavior.
