# Product brief

EntryLoop is an offline-first event check-in and synchronization platform. Its defining experiment is reconciling admissions among independent entrances by showing and scanning QR batches without internet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or a central server during exchange.

## Phase 1 success

Two genuinely disconnected physical devices create real local records, exchange QR codes through their cameras and converge to the same operation IDs, attendance and conflicts after receiving the same valid operations. A successful browser/image test is supporting evidence; physical acceptance remains a separate gate.

## Scope implemented

Two synthetic events, twelve synthetic tickets per event, random scanner identity per explicit browser database profile, immutable admissions, deterministic merge, visible conflict evidence, IndexedDB persistence, bounded single-frame QR batches, schema/hash/event validation, real QR generation and image/camera adapters, installable PWA configuration, minimal responsive engineering workbench and automated tests.

## Non-goals

No production event administration, ticket issuing, attendee data, dashboard product, authentication, cloud sync, central server, elaborate identity or marketing page. Local duplicate rejection cannot become a claim of global simultaneous duplicate prevention.
