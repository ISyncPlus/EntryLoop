## Accessibility correction boundaries

The existing visual identity/layout stays. Changes are limited to inherited product theme backgrounds/foregrounds/borders and necessary storage/recovery/update controls. Existing native controls, focus handling, ticket QR black/white contrast and signed-operation security remain. No animations, agency template, new font, marketing section or design dependency was added.

Automated contrast checks are evidence for the tested elements/themes, not a comprehensive WCAG audit, screen-reader certification, mobile-camera validation or proof that the owner's particular device now works. See RELIABILITY_TEST_RESULTS.md for executed results and blockers.

## Stage 4 backlog after reliability clearance

1. Build a short organizer-led enrollment/provisioning flow with a clear device handoff and immediate confirmation of the correct event/workspace. Design the trust ceremony with the security engineer; do not make fingerprint comparison cosmetic.
2. Give cameras stable, readable QR targets and explicit next/received/retry feedback. Measure supported Mac/phone cameras and preserve authentic payload verification through every fallback.
3. Reduce operator-facing terminology; show technical evidence progressively. Distinguish organizing, prepared scanning and actual event-scoped authority.
4. Make synchronization direction, incomplete transfers, return acknowledgments and conflict investigation understandable without reading protocol documentation.
5. Make persistent local context and recovery limits visible at the moment they matter. Explain what a records backup contains, why private keys are absent and why an absent key cannot be recreated.
6. Review focus order, error/status announcements, touch targets, dark/light contrast and real device layout. Do not represent automated browser tests as physical acceptance.

Deployment, cloud transport, key backup/rotation, Stage 3 and full Stage 4 are not part of this hardening task.
